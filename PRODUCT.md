# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Greenfield. User decision: plain static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework, no build step) deployed to GitHub Pages. Chosen for free hosting, zero maintenance burden for a non-technical church, and the user's strength in hand-written HTML/CSS. Delegated to this recommendation after user accepted the "GitHub Pages (free)" default.

## Users

- **Primary:** First-time and returning visitors in Lekki/Lagos — new movers, seekers, and neighbours — arriving on desktop or mobile, often via a shared link or a Google search for "church near me Lekki." State of mind: curious, possibly anxious about walking into a new church; they need to know what to expect and feel welcomed before they visit.
- **Secondary:** Current members — checking service times, submitting prayer requests, catching up on sermons, finding ministry info.
- **Tertiary:** Giving partners and the church admin — using the Give page and Contact page.

## Product Purpose

MercyLand Parish's public website: the front door that turns "I drive past it on Lekki-Epe Expressway" into "I'm coming this Sunday." It answers the visitor's first questions (when, where, what to expect, how to visit, how to give/pray) and makes the church feel warm, real, and worth showing up to.

## Positioning

A young, warm parish in the Lekki Atlantic Garden estate — physically rooted in a specific Lagos address, not a generic "church template." Its truth a neighbouring product cannot copy: the exact Lekki location, the real service rhythm (Sunday 7am–12pm window; midweek Tue & Thu 6:30–8pm), and a genuinely welcoming, light, modern voice rather than the austere/traditional church-site default.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Confirmed:** 9 content sections (Home, About/Our Story, Service Times & Location, Sermons/Messages, Ministries, Events, Give, Prayer Requests, Contact). English only. Giving = info-only page (bank details + donation-link slot), no live payment backend. Sermons = text/notes placeholders structured for later audio/video embeds. Brand assets = tasteful placeholders with clearly-marked replacement slots.
- **Undecided (recorded, not invented):** Exact Sunday service start times; real logo / building / pastor / congregation photography; Paystack or other live-donation provider (deferred); custom domain (deferred).
- **Binding constraints:** No paid services. Must deploy free on GitHub Pages. Must be maintainable by a non-developer church admin later. Must be responsive (mobile-first, Lagos audiences are heavily mobile).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **MercyLand Parish** (keep exact spelling and capitalisation).
- Voice: warm, friendly, light, welcoming — modern, not archaic. No prosperity-hype or fear-based language.
- No invented testimonials, attendance figures, founder names, or history. All such copy ships as clearly-marked placeholders on the church's replacement list.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: church name, phone, full address, service-hour schedule.
- Absent (must NOT be fabricated): founding story/history, pastor name & bio, slogans/mission text beyond the name, photos, logo, sermon archive, event list, ministry descriptions, social links, map coordinates (address is real; a map embed can be derived from the address).

## Product Principles

1. Welcoming over impressive — a visitor should feel "I can walk in" within 5 seconds.
2. Findable fast — service times, location, and "plan your visit" must be one tap from anywhere.
3. Honest placeholders — every gap is visibly marked for the church to fill, never silently invented.
4. Light, calm, warm — the visual world earns the "inviting" brief without sliding into generic AI-cream-and-terracotta slop.
5. Free & maintainable — no recurring cost, no developer required for routine updates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast ≥4.5:1 body text, keyboard-navigable nav/forms, focus rings themed from palette, real semantic HTML, labelled form fields for Prayer Requests and Contact.

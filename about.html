Our story

MercyLand Parish is a church family in Lekki Atlantic Garden, built on a simple belief: everyone is welcome at the table.

Who we are

MercyLand Parish exists to be a place of mercy, worship, and belonging in the heart of Lekki. We are a community of ordinary people who have found grace — and we'd love for you to find it too.

What we believe

We hold to the historic Christian faith — that God is love, Jesus is Lord, and the Spirit is at work today. We teach the Bible clearly and live it kindly.

Our Story & Vision

MercyLand Parish is a church family in Lekki Atlantic Garden, built on a simple belief: everyone is welcome at the table of God's grace.

Who We Are

MercyLand Parish exists to be a place of mercy, worship, and belonging in the heart of Lekki. We are a community of believers who have found grace — and we'd love for you to experience God's fullness with us.

As part of the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG), we are committed to righteous living, fervent prayer, Bible-centered spiritual growth, and making heaven while impacting our local community.

What We Believe

We hold to the biblical Christian faith — that God is love, Jesus Christ is Lord and Savior, and the Holy Spirit is actively empowering believers today. We preach the uncompromised Word of God with love and practical relevance.

Our Heart & Mission

We want MercyLand to feel like home: warm and uplifting on Sundays, supportive through the week, and generous in caring for families and our neighborhood across Lekki and Lagos.
